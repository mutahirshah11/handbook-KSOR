## The return window

A customer may return an item **within 30 days**, and must have the
receipt.[^owner-2026-09-13]

The item must be **unused**.[^owner-2026-09-13]

A **receipt is the only accepted proof of purchase**. Without one there is no
return — no other evidence is accepted in its place, and staff do not look the
purchase up.[^owner-2026-09-13]

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are **exempt from the 30-day window**, and that exemption has **no
outer limit**: a faulty item may be returned at any point after
purchase.[^owner-2026-09-13]

Whether an item is faulty is decided by **a staff member inspecting it when it
is returned**. The customer reports the fault; the inspection at the counter
settles it.[^owner-2026-09-13]

Two conditions still differ for a faulty item:

- The **receipt is still required**, exactly as for any other
  return.[^owner-2026-09-13]
- The **unused condition is waived** — a faulty item has usually been used, and
  that does not refuse the return.[^owner-2026-09-13]

## When the money arrives

A refund reaches the customer **within 5 working days**, counted from the day
**we receive the returned item**.[^owner-2026-09-13]

A refund is issued to the customer's **original payment method** — the card or
account they paid with. No other form is offered: not store credit, not a
voucher, not an exchange.[^owner-2026-09-13]
